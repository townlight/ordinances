Chapter 13.40 Keeping Livestock

13.40.010 Owner Responsibilities.
All livestock keepers must meet the provisions of Title 13 and applicable administrative rules. The keeping of livestock must not create a nuisance or disturb neighboring residents due to noise, odor, damage, or threats to public health.

13.40.020 Backyard Livestock.
Up to four chickens, ducks, pigeons and/or other similarly sized domestic fowl may be kept on any lot. Up to six small domestic fowl may be kept on lots 10,000 square feet and greater. It is unlawful to have or keep roosters except for agricultural purposes on lots that allow agricultural uses.

13.40.030 Large Livestock.
Large livestock may only be kept on lots 20,000 square feet or greater that allow agricultural uses through Portland City Code Title 33 Zoning, or that have an approved conditional use.
